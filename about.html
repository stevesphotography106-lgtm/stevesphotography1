ABOUT STEVE

Focused on the
aircraft.

Hello, my name is Stephen, or Steve. I am an aviation photographer based in Northeastern Virginia, just 45 miles south of Washington, D.C. I primarily focus on military aviation, capturing aircraft such as UH-60 Black Hawks, C-17 Globemaster IIIs, and the occasional military fighter jet.

While I also enjoy photographing civilian aviation, I have always had a stronger interest in military aircraft. The variety of aircraft, unique designs, and environments they operate in make military aviation especially interesting to me. Whether it is a Black Hawk flying overhead, a C-17 on approach, or a fighter jet passing through the area, I enjoy capturing these aircraft and sharing the moments that catch my eye.

THE APPROACH

Every frame is a record of an aircraft doing what it was built to do.
